There was this new age flute duo only active in the 80’s called [Emerald Web](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esYqE7x3l9I) that's been a major influence of mine while getting back into the flute. I highlight one of their albums on my Bandcamp page, and the [only living member](https://www.katepple.com/) found me, bought my music, and left a wonderful message that she signed, “It’s nice to know Emerald Web’s music has created some ripples in the pool of life on earth. Keep playing and creating ripples.” The flute world proves small.
