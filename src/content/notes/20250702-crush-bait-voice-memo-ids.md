Crush Bait is imbued with a period of my life where I experienced a rush of renewed optimism around the endless potential for serendipitious connection. At the time that First Birthday came out it was my favorite thing I had ever made.

The last thing I added were these voice memos from actual moments in my life. Most were significant in their banality, some in their novelty. They were all moments in which I felt at home in my life.

- palm springs tram ride (2024)
- walking from studio to the G train with lucy and sam (2025)
- sam giving me a soldering pep talk from a uhaul with AWS (2025)
- laughing at texts from dani and lee (2025)
- blowing out the candles at my 29th birthday party (2024)
- tempelhofer with anna (2020)
- lunch in paris with sam and renée (2023)
- lucy and AWS’s city hall wedding (2023)
